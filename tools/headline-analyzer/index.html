    How Our Headline Analyzer Works

    Writing catchy titles is both an art and a science. Our algorithm evaluates your headlines based on four essential pillars of search performance and reader psychology:

    📏

    Length Check

    Flags titles that are too short to attract attention or too long, avoiding risky search engine result truncation (recommending 50-60 characters or under 600 pixels).

    ⚖️

    Word Balance

    Scans your copy for a strategic blend of common, uncommon, emotional, and power words ("ultimate", "proven", "secret") to capture click intent.

    🎭

    Sentiment Analysis

    Categorizes the emotional tone of your title as positive, negative, or neutral. Headlines with clear sentiment consistently score higher CTRs.

    🎯

    Search Intent

    Validates your title structure against top-ranking SERP patterns (lists, questions, guides) to guarantee search relevance and alignment.

    • Keep it between 40-60 characters for optimal SEO
    • Use power words like "ultimate", "essential", "proven"
    • Include numbers for listicles (e.g., "7 Ways...")
    • Add emotional words to create engagement
    • Use "How to" format for tutorials
    • Ask questions to spark curiosity

    How do I check my blog title for SEO?

    What is a headline score checker?

    A headline score checker is a specialized analysis tool that rates the effectiveness of headlines on a scale from 0 to 100. A score of 70 or higher is considered 'good', indicating that your headline has a strong blend of keywords, optimal length, emotional triggers, and high expected click-through rates (CTR).

    How does an SEO title checker improve search performance?

    An SEO title checker free allows you to identify formatting and psychological issues before publishing. It ensures your title tag doesn't get cut off in Google SERPs and optimizes the emotional tone, helping your pages rank higher and earn more organic clicks.

    Is there a difference between a blog title analyzer and an ad headline analyzer?

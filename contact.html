Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you hit send.

How quickly do you reply?

FreeClipboard is maintained by one person, so reply times vary. Most messages receive a response within 1–3 business days.

How should I report a broken tool?

Include the tool URL, what you clicked, the result you expected, and what actually happened. Browser and device details help a lot. Screenshots are a bonus.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Absolutely. The best suggestions explain a real problem, who it affects, and why existing tools on the site don't already solve it.

Do you offer paid accounts or custom plans?

No paid subscription plans exist right now. FreeClipboard is a free, ad-supported tools site.

Which email should I use?

Both support@freeclipboard.com and freeclipboard@gmail.com are monitored. Either works fine — or just use the contact form above.
